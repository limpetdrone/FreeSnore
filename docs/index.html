Comprehensive Feature Suite

Everything Built for Quiet, Restful Nights

From cutting-edge acoustic detection to outdoor pollen tracking, storage optimization, and clinical PDF exports.

Acoustic Snore Detection

Filters ambient bedroom rustles and movement using respiratory periodicity and duration gating to isolate authentic snoring episodes.

Fall-Asleep Delay

Microphone activates quietly upfront adhering to iOS background rules, while acoustic evaluation begins once you're asleep.

Overnight Sound Timeline

Inspect loudness spikes and snoring duration minute-by-minute across the entire night with interactive playback and scrub bar.

Home Screen Widgets

Glance at last night's snore score, sound duration, and your active streak right from your home screen with native iOS SwiftUI & Android AppWidget extensions.

Apple Health & Health Connect

Correlate your snoring intensity with pulse rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), heart rate variability (HRV), respiration rate, and sleep stages without manual logging.

Pollen & Outdoor Air Quality

Correlates night-time breathing with Open-Meteo & CAMS outdoor data: tracks birch, grass, and ragweed pollen, humidity, PM2.5, and air pressure.

Goal Streaks & Lifestyle Trends

Set custom quiet goals and build streaks. Analyze weekday vs. weekend patterns to see how late nights or alcohol impact your rest.

Native Audio Compaction

Keeps only the snoring parts of older nights (80MB trimmed to 5-15MB) via lossless native audio trimming, keeping all your statistics intact.

Clinical Doctor PDF Report

Generate clean clinical PDF reports for doctor consultations, with an in-app interactive preview before sending or printing.

OLED Anti-Burn Night Clock & Alarm Sync

Pure black dimmed clock mode with minute-by-minute pixel shift to protect your OLED screen, plus automatic session finalization when your morning alarm rings.

Effortless Setup

How It Works

Only 3 steps from setting your phone down to reviewing your morning report.

01

1. Set Delay & Start

Choose your fall-asleep delay (e.g., 15 mins), tap your applied factors or remedies, and place your phone on the nightstand.

02

2. Sleep Soundly

FreeSnore silently tracks your nighttime sound patterns while you lock your screen or use the dimmed night clock.

03

3. Morning Review

Wake up and view your Snore Score, loudness breakdown, audio snippets, and exportable doctor PDF report.
